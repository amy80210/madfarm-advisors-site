Privacy Policy

At Madfarm Advisors, we are committed to maintaining the confidentiality and security of the personal and financial information entrusted to us by our clients. This privacy policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect sensitive information related to business transactions for buyers and sellers. We understand the importance of privacy in such matters and handle all client information with the highest level of discretion and care.

Information we collect

In the course of providing our services, we may collect certain details from you, such as contact information and data pertinent to the valuation and acquisition process. This information helps us to facilitate communications, manage transactions, and deliver personalized service aligned with your needs and goals.

When you use the contact form on this website, we receive the name, email address, and message you provide, along with your phone number and company if you choose to include them. We use this information only to respond to your inquiry. This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies.

How we share information

We do not share this information with third parties, except in instances where collaboration with essential service providers is necessary for transaction facilitation or when legally obligated.

How we protect information

To protect your data, we employ industry-standard security measures aimed at preventing unauthorized access. While we are diligent in safeguarding your information, please note that no security system is entirely foolproof. Therefore, we commit to continuously updating our practices to uphold the highest standards of security.

Changes to this policy

Madfarm Advisors may occasionally update this policy to reflect changes in our privacy practices. Any updates will be posted here, and continued use of our services implies acceptance of these changes.

Contact

For any questions or concerns regarding this privacy policy, please contact us at info@madfarm-advisors.com.
