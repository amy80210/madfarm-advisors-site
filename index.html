The Process

Structurally rigorous. Strategically flexible.

Preparation is fixed: the same discipline on every engagement, in the same order. Market strategy is built from what that preparation reveals. Two halves of one process, and they are not interchangeable.

Foundation

Goals and process meetings, the data room build, and financial and operations discovery. Short, dense, and the single biggest driver of your launch date.

Preparation

Quality of earnings, the financial workbook, market analysis and go-to-market design. We build the file a buyer will underwrite from before any buyer sees it.

Market

Tiered buyer outreach, NDAs and CIM release, management meetings, and the IOI round through LOI selection. Strategy is an output of preparation, not a template applied to it.

Close

Confirmatory diligence, definitive agreements, net working capital true-up and funds flow. Diligence should confirm known facts, not discover new ones.
