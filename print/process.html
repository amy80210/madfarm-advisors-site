In Detail

What actually happens, phase by phase.

01

EvaluationComplimentary · pre-engagement

A discovery session and a preliminary read on value — what the business could command, what a process would look like, and what to address first. No cost, no obligation.

02

FoundationKickoff

Goals, data room build, and financial and operational discovery. Short and dense — it sets everything downstream in motion.

03

PreparationThe core

Financial preparation, quality of earnings, market analysis and go-to-market design. The heaviest lifting of the engagement, and what a clean process is built on.

04

MarketIn market

Tiered buyer outreach, NDAs and CIM, management meetings, then indications of interest and LOI selection. We name your buyer list from evidence — not on day one.

05

CloseThe finish

Confirmatory diligence, definitive agreement, working-capital true-up and funds flow. Thorough preparation is why diligence confirms what’s known instead of uncovering surprises.

We set the pace, and buyers react to it. In a sell-side process, tempo is leverage — and we don’t hand it to the other side of the table.

03 · What You Receive

Six deliverables, all yours.

Produced during preparation, used through close, and yours to keep afterward.

01

Financial workbook

Normalized P&L, adjusted EBITDA, cash flow, customer analysis and net working capital — the underwriting file.

02

Deal-ready data room

Structured, populated, access-controlled and organized the way a buyer’s diligence team works.

03

Confidential information memorandum

The full narrative and financial case for the business, released only under NDA.

04

Blind teaser

An anonymized one-page profile used for every first approach.

05

Buyer list

Named, tiered and researched — built from market evidence, not a database pull.

06

Go-to-market strategy

Buyer mix, sequencing, pacing, process type and the outreach plan.

Confidentiality

Your business never appears on a marketplace.

Confidentiality is a design decision, not a disclaimer. Every approach is deliberate — made by us, to a named buyer — and nothing that identifies your business goes out until it has to.

Who gets read in, and when, stays your call.
