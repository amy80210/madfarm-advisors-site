Senior Leadership
Founder & Managing Partner

Spencer Williams

Spencer is the founding principal of Madfarm and brings more than a decade of transactional and operating experience to every engagement. He leads sell-side and strategic advisory mandates hands-on, from first conversation to close. He is collegial at the table, firm and straightforward on terms, and data-driven in how he builds and defends a deal.

He began his career in private legal practice in Denver after earning his Juris Doctorate with a focus on business transactions. Three years later he launched a consulting practice advising buyers and asset owners in Colorado’s complex water and natural resources markets, and led the development of database products used to underwrite numerous industrial services transactions across Texas and New Mexico. He then managed and scaled a tech-enabled industrial logistics and supply chain business within a larger venture-backed startup.

In late 2020 he returned to his transactional roots and founded Madfarm to serve lower middle market business owners exclusively. He holds his J.D. from the University of Tennessee College of Law and is an active member of the Alliance of Mergers & Acquisitions Advisors.

Managing Director

Will Davis

Will joined Madfarm to give lower middle market business owners a seller-first advocate who knows what actually drives enterprise value, not just how to run a process. He brings more than a decade of transaction experience to every engagement, fully aligned with the outcome the founder is working toward.

His edge comes from three perspectives most bankers never combine: sales leadership that scaled a startup from $0 to $100M in revenue, hands-on experience as a private equity deal sponsor, and direct execution on capital raises and sponsor operating initiatives. It is the difference between an advisor who manages a transaction and a partner who understands how growth builds value, how buyers underwrite risk, and how to structure a deal that puts leverage back in the seller’s hands.

He holds a B.A. from Texas A&M University and an MBA from the McCombs School of Business at the University of Texas at Austin, and still bleeds maroon through and through.
