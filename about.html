About Madfarm

A practice built to sell mission-critical businesses.

We are not a broker. We are not a generalist boutique. We are a senior-led sell-side practice for owners who take their business seriously and expect the same of the people who represent it.

The Madfarm Thesis

Boutique attention. Institutional results.

Madfarm is a lower middle market investment bank built to sell mission-critical businesses — industrials, commercial services, software, and professional services in the $5M–$50M revenue range.

Every engagement is led by the principal who pitched it. No handoffs. No learning curves on your dime. We use modern tools to run a sharper process, and we treat every deal as a people deal — because that’s what it actually is.

Owners hire us because they want the rigor of a real M&A process and the relational fluency to navigate what actually happens — across the table, around the table, and inside their own business.

Senior Leadership
Founder & Managing Partner

Spencer Williams

Spencer founded Madfarm to give lower middle market owners the senior representation their transactions deserve. He brings over a decade of transactional and operating experience to every engagement — and personally leads each mandate from pitch to close.

His background spans three perspectives most bankers have only one of: operating leadership in industrial and natural-resource service markets, transaction advisory across the lower middle market, and legal training in business transactions. That combination is the difference between a banker who reads from a playbook and an advisor who can navigate what actually happens when a deal gets hard.

He holds a Juris Doctorate in business transactions from the University of Tennessee College of Law and is an active member of the Alliance of Mergers & Acquisitions Advisors.

Focus

What we do — and what we don’t.

Discipline is a form of respect. We take on the work we’re built to run well, and we say so plainly.

Sell-side M&A advisory
