Defending Value in Bio-Pharma Engineering

The Situation

Engineering a specialized succession.

The owner-operator of a premier engineering firm specializing in bio-pharma manufacturing facilities sought a path to retirement. While the company was uniquely positioned within the high-growth GLP-1 pharmaceutical production sector, the path to an exit was complicated by a challenging internal partnership.

The seller required an advisor who could professionally navigate sensitive partner dynamics while articulating the firm’s complex technical value to a broad buyer pool.

The Challenge

Beyond the surface.

Financial Reconstruction

The firm operated on cash-basis accounting with an atypical compensation structure. To make the business “transactable” for a sophisticated buyer, we recommended ancillary accounting support to validate the true financial position.

A Mid-Diligence “Black Swan”

At the 11th hour, a key offer was temporarily withdrawn. Despite the volatility and the loss of a major customer, we re-leveraged the firm’s niche bio-pharma expertise to defend a premium valuation.

Internal Friction

We acted as a “shield” between the buyer and an uncooperative, absentee stakeholder — keeping the transaction professional and on schedule despite significant internal antagonism.

The Boutique Strategy

Competitive tension & technical advocacy.

Broad market reach paired with high-touch advocacy — to protect the seller’s legacy and valuation.

Broad-Auction Execution

We researched and targeted approximately 150 potential buyers — from PE-backed platforms to national strategics — resulting in 8 IOIs and 2 aggressive finalists.

Technical Value Positioning

A deep dive into the platform calibrated its exact fit within the pharmaceutical marketplace. By framing the firm as a mission-critical link in the GLP-1 supply chain, we sustained high demand for the asset.

Hands-On Coaching

We kept the seller in the “driver’s seat” throughout — constant coaching on the nuances of the sale so the owner could run the business without interruption.

The Results

A full exit — value protected under pressure.
